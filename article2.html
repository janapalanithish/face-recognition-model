Face Detection vs Face Recognition — Key Differences Explained

Many people confuse face detection and face recognition, but they serve different purposes. Understanding this difference is important when working with AI-based systems.

What is Face Detection?

Face detection is the process of identifying whether a human face exists in an image or video. It does not determine identity. It simply answers: "Is there a face here?" This is commonly used in cameras and photo apps to locate faces.

What is Face Recognition?

Face recognition goes beyond detection. It identifies or verifies a person by comparing their facial features with stored data. This process involves analyzing unique patterns such as the distance between eyes and shape of facial structure.
