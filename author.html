The Mission Behind RecognizeMe AI

I created RecognizeMe AI as more than just a tool. In an era where biometric data is increasingly sensitive, I wanted to build a platform that demonstrates the power of Client-Side AI. By processing face recognition entirely in the user's browser, I aim to show that we can leverage advanced technology without compromising personal privacy.
