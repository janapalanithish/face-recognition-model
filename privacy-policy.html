Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FaceAI handles your data when you use our face recognition tool. Your privacy is important, and we are committed to protecting your information.

2. Data We Collect

We may process webcam video streams and facial data for detection purposes. This data is used only for real-time processing and is not stored unless explicitly registered by the user.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to provide face detection and recognition functionality. We do not sell, share, or distribute your data to third parties.

4. Firebase Data Storage

If you choose to register your face, the system stores a mathematical representation (face descriptor) in Firebase. This data can be deleted upon request.

5. Biometric Data Notice

Facial data is considered sensitive biometric information. We handle this data securely and only use it for the intended purpose of recognition within the application.

6. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes.

7. Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (by Google) for database services. These services have their own privacy policies which you can review separately.

8. User Rights

You have the right to request access, modification, or deletion of your data at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

This tool is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
