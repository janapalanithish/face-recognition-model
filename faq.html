Frequently Asked Questions

Is my face data stored permanently?

No. Data is only stored if you register your face manually. Otherwise, it is not saved.

Do I need an account?

No account is required. You can use the tool instantly.

Is this tool free?

Yes, the tool is completely free to use.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, but performance depends on browser and device camera quality.

Is my webcam recorded?

No, video is processed in real-time and not recorded.

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on lighting, camera quality, and face visibility.

How can I delete my data?

You can remove stored data from Firebase or reset the database.

Why does it need camera access?

The tool requires camera access to detect and analyze faces in real time.
